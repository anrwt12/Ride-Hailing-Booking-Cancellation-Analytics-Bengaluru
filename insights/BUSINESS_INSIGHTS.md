🎯 Business Recommendations
Priority 1 — Reduce Driver Cancellations 🚨
Driver cancellations are the largest cancellation category.
Investigate why drivers cancel.
Improve driver availability.
Monitor cancellation patterns by location and hour.
Priority 2 — Improve High-Failure Locations 📍

Focus first on locations with:

High booking volume
Above-average failure rate

Especially locations such as Vijayanagar, Kengeri, Hosur Road and Tumkur Road.

Priority 3 — Improve Peak-Hour Supply ⏰

Pay special attention to:

08:00
12:00

Ensure enough drivers are available during these periods.

Priority 4 — Investigate Poor Routes 🛣️

Focus on routes with:

High booking volume
Success rate below 70%
Priority 5 — Retain Repeat Customers ⭐
Identify high-value repeat customers.
Create loyalty strategies.
Monitor their booking success rate.
Encourage repeat usage.
